# Code of conduct

This is a small repository. What follows is short because a long document that
promises a process nobody staffs is worse than a short one that is kept.

## What is expected

Talk about the work. Disagree about the code, the evidence and the decision,
and take the disagreement to what was actually written rather than to the
person who wrote it.

Bring evidence. A claim about behaviour comes with the command or the steps
that produced it. "This is wrong" is a start; "this is wrong, here is the run"
is a contribution.

Assume the other person is trying. Most defects are somebody's careful work
meeting a case they did not have.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, or attacks on a person rather than their work. Sexual
attention or imagery. Publishing anyone's private information. Persisting after
being asked to stop.

This holds in issues, pull requests, commit messages and anywhere else this
project's work happens.

## What happens

The maintainer of this repository handles it. Report by email to the address on
the maintainer's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's own abuse reporting if the
maintainer is the problem.

Expect an acknowledgement within seven days. What follows is a conversation, an
edit or deletion of the content, or a block from the repository, depending on
what happened. There is no committee, no appeal process and no published
timeline beyond the acknowledgement, because there is one maintainer and
inventing more would be a promise rather than a plan.

A report about a security vulnerability goes to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead; this document is about conduct.
